# Book Club Archive: Project Vision

## 1. Overview
This project is a clean, accessible website designed to track, rank, and display the books our club has read. Rather than using cluttered public review apps, we want a simple, highly polished space focused entirely on our group's reading history and scoring.

---

## 2. Core Principles
* **Zero Friction (No Accounts):** There are no logins, passwords, or user profiles. Anyone with the link can immediately view the rankings and score breakdowns.
* **Polished Design:** The site should feel clean, intentional, and well-organized, working seamlessly on both desktop and mobile screens.
* **Transparent Scoring:** A single average score doesn't tell the whole story. The platform emphasizes individual perspectives by showing exactly how each member rated a book.

---

## 3. Core Experience

### The Leaderboard (Homepage)
The homepage acts as a straightforward leaderboard, showing all the books we've read sorted from highest to lowest collective average score.
* **At-a-Glance Metrics:** Each row clearly displays the book's title, author, and overall average rating.
* **Cover Art:** Books are presented alongside thumbnails of their front covers. Simple, clean placeholders will be used until the official cover graphics are uploaded.

### The Book Detail View
Clicking any book on the leaderboard opens a dedicated page focused on the group's rating breakdown.
* **Primary Details:** Displays a larger view of the book cover, author information, and the final average score.
* **Individual Score Breakdown:** The core feature of this page is a clear list showing every participating member's name alongside the specific score they awarded. This makes it instantly obvious whether a book was universally liked or heavily debated.

---

## 4. Design Direction
* **Clean & Readable:** Prioritize whitespace and legible typography over complex UI elements or heavy layouts.
* **Visual Focus on Content:** Use neutral background tones that allow the book covers and ratings to stand out as the primary visual elements.
* **Intentional Placeholders:** Temporary cover graphics should look neat and fit the site's aesthetic naturally, avoiding the look of broken or missing images.
